## Reading Pagoda

Built in 1908 as part of a planned resort, the Pagoda became city property in 1911. Its hillside setting makes it one of Reading’s most recognizable landmarks.

## GoggleWorks Center for the Arts

A former goggle factory became a community arts campus when GoggleWorks opened in 2005. Today its studios, galleries and classes connect Reading’s industrial past with its creative community.

## Santander Performing Arts Center

The building began in the 1870s as a market with a Masonic Temple above. It later served as the Academy of Music and became a venue for vaudeville, movies and live performances.

## Francis Hall

Alvernia’s oldest campus building was dedicated by the Bernardine Franciscan Sisters in 1926. Originally an orphanage, it later became a high school and then the home of Alvernia College in 1958. Its patio, theatre and art spaces make it a campus landmark.

Description source: https://www.alvernia.edu/about/francis-hall
